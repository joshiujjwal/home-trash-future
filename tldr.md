# Household trash: TLDR

**An open question, not a proposed solution:** Can we engineer away the friction of sorting, or are motivation and infrastructure equally important? Could AI, robotics, and vision help?

[Full research and citations](url.md) | [Website](https://joshiujjwal.github.io/home-trash-future/)

## What the evidence says

- **Waste:** In 2018, 50% of U.S. municipal solid waste was landfilled and 32.1% recycled or composted. These figures include residential and commercial/institutional waste, not households alone. [EPA national overview](https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling/national-overview-facts-and-figures-materials).
- **Climate:** Food represents about 24% of landfilled municipal waste by weight but an estimated 58% of fugitive methane from U.S. municipal landfills. This is a modeled estimate, not a share of all U.S. methane. [EPA, 2023](https://www.epa.gov/land-research/quantifying-methane-emissions-landfilled-food-waste).
- **Behavior:** A Northern Ireland study found confusion, competing priorities, and expectations of easier recycling alongside environmental concern. It does not establish that most people "don't care." [PLOS ONE, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9064103/).
- **AI:** Vision-guided sorting is deployed at industrial facilities. That is not proof of an affordable home sorter that handles every mixed item. [Fortune, 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/06/26/ai-recycling-trash-amp-robotics/).
- **Beyond disposal:** Preventing wasted food can avoid production impacts that downstream treatment does much less to offset. [EPA, From Field to Bin, 2023](https://www.epa.gov/land-research/field-bin-environmental-impacts-us-food-waste-management-pathways).

## Still open

How much would lower effort change participation? Where should sorting happen? What are the total costs and emissions once electricity, consumables, collection, and the recovered material's destination are included?

## Current options

Examples, not recommendations. Product descriptions are manufacturer claims unless noted; listings checked November 21, 2026.

| Option | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Mill](https://www.mill.com/) | Processes food scraps into dried grounds, not mixed-trash sorting. |
| [Lomi 3](https://lomi.com/products/lomi-3-food-recycler) | Countertop food processing; manufacturer describes the output as pre-compost. |
| [TOWNEW](https://mytownew.com/) | Automatic bag sealing/changing, not material sorting; linked source is a product roundup. |
| [Laundry Jet](https://laundryjet.com/) | Clothing transport, not a demonstrated mixed-waste system. |
| [Envac](https://www.envacgroup.com/) | Larger-scale pneumatic waste infrastructure reference; current details not verified. |
| [AMP](https://fortune.com/2025/06/26/ai-recycling-trash-amp-robotics/) | Industrial AI sorting, not a consumer home appliance. |

Local collection, ordinary bins, and home composting are relevant comparisons too. Volume reduction alone does not establish landfill diversion or lower emissions.
